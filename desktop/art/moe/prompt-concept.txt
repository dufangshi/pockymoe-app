Original mascot character design sheet for a developer desktop app called "Remote Codex". The character is "Moe": a tiny round baby axolotl-like spirit, cobalt blue body (#2F5BEA family) with a cream belly, three soft frilly gills on each side of the head glowing warm amber at the tips, stubby little limbs, short tail, big glossy dark eyes with white highlights, gentle smile. Cute, calm and reassuring. Clean 2D vector-like cel shading, crisp dark-navy outline of even weight, flat colors with one shadow tone, no texture. Show the same character 6 times in a neat 3x2 grid on a plain pure white background, consistent proportions: 1 front idle floating, 2 waving, 3 typing on a tiny glowing terminal (working), 4 raising a little arm with a small amber exclamation bubble (needs attention), 5 droopy sad with a tiny red x (error), 6 jumping with joy. No text, no logo, no watermark.
